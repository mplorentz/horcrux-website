1. The Service

Horcrux is a mobile app that backs up your most sensitive credentials in vaults with encrypted keys that are distributed to people you choose ("stewards"). When you or a steward needs to recover your data, a threshold of stewards approve the recovery and your data is decrypted.

Horcrux also operates a relay and supporting services (push notifications, account and consent management) to make the Service usable.
