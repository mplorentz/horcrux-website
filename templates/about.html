About

Horcrux was started by Matt Lorentz in 2025 supported by a small group of friends and a generous grant from OpenSats.org.

Origin story

Matt was initially inspired by the Dark Crystal project and their tool for backing up identity keys on the Secure Scuttlebutt network. For years he dreamed with friends about a way to mutually share server passwords in order to wind things down gracefully if the worst should happen.

Horcrux was born from those conversations — a practical answer to a question that haunts anyone who runs critical infrastructure alone: what happens to it if something happens to me?

A convivial tool

Horcrux is designed to be a convivial tool: free to use, open source, part of the commons of the open web, made to outlast the makers.

It doesn't lock you into a platform. It doesn't monetise your trust network. It doesn't disappear when a startup runs out of venture funding. The code is open source and always will be — anyone can run it, audit it, or adapt it.
