About

Horcrux was started by Matt Lorentz in 2025 supported by a small group of friends and a generous grant from OpenSats.org. Matt was initially inspired by the Dark Crystal project and their tool for backing up identity keys on the Secure Scuttlebutt network. For years he dreamed with friends about a way to mutually share server passwords in order to wind things down gracefully if the worst should happen.

Horcrux is designed to be a convivial tool: free to use, open source, part of commons of the open web, made to outlast the makers. If you would like to contribute with constructive feedback, encouragement, code, money, or your own skills, please do get in touch via our contact form.
