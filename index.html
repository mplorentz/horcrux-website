Horcrux Backup

Store secrets with friends and family.

Horcrux uses Shamir's Secret Sharing to split your most sensitive data - passwords, cryptographic keys, digital wills - into encrypted fragments distributed to people you choose. No single person holds the key. No cloud stores your data. But when you need to recover it, your stewards can put the pieces back together.

Free and open source. Horcrux is generously funded by OpenSats.org
