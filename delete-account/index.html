Delete Your Account

Permanently remove your data from Horcrux

Horcrux stores a Nostr key pair on your device. This key pair is your identity — it's how you authenticate with stewards and how your vaults are associated with you. Deleting your account removes your public key from our records and severs your association with any vaults you own or steward.

What happens when you delete your account

Your Nostr key pair is removed — The public key associated with your account will be deleted from our systems. Your private key is already stored only on your device and can be removed by uninstalling the app.

Your vaults remain on stewards' devices — Encrypted shards held by your stewards are stored on their devices, not on our servers. Those shards are still encrypted and unreadable without a formal recovery process.

Steward associations are removed — Vaults you were stewarding for others will no longer have you listed as a steward. Those vault owners should be notified to find a replacement steward.

Recovery requests are cancelled — Any active recovery requests associated with your account will be invalidated.

What we need from you

To process your deletion request, please fill out the form below. We need your email address so we can confirm the request belongs to you. If you know your Nostr public key (npub...), including it will help us locate your account faster.

We'll process your request within 30 days and send a confirmation email once it's complete.

Before you go

Before requesting deletion, consider exporting any data you may need. Vault contents and recovery information stored only in the Horcrux app should be backed up separately.

If you're unsure whether deletion is what you need, reach out on GitHub and we'll help.
